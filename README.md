# Impasto

**Pizza dough, planned backwards from bake time.**

Impasto is a pizza dough calculator and fermentation planner for home bakers. Tell it **when you want to eat**, choose your pizza style and dough method, and Impasto works backwards to build the recipe and schedule every important step.

## What Impasto does

Most pizza dough calculators stop at baker's percentages. Impasto also turns the recipe into a **real clock-time plan**.

Choose your bake time and Impasto works backwards to calculate and schedule:

- ingredient quantities and baker's percentages
- fermentation timing
- preferment timing
- mixing and dough-development guidance
- water temperature for the target dough temperature
- room-temperature and cold-fermentation stages
- dividing, balling, panning, rolling and baking
- reminders for hands-on steps
- final-proof adjustments through **Proof Check**

The idea is simple: make the dough fit your schedule, rather than making your schedule fit the dough.

## Dough methods

Impasto supports:

- **Direct dough**
- **Poolish**
- **Biga**
- **Sourdough**

Preferments and sourdough are integrated into the same backwards-planning system, so the whole process is scheduled around the time you want to bake.

## Fermentation pace

Three pace options make it easy to choose how much time you want to give the dough:

- **Quick** — same-day routes where appropriate
- **Classic** — overnight or medium-length fermentation
- **Flavour** — longer fermentation for more development

Timelines use real clock times and distinguish between passive waiting and the moments when you actually need to do something.

## Dough temperature and mixing

Impasto calculates the water temperature needed to target the appropriate final dough temperature.

It also adapts guidance to how you mix:

- **By hand**
- **Stand mixer**
- **Food processor**

The selected method affects both the water-temperature calculation and the mixing instructions.

## Proof Check

Kitchen temperature at the start of a recipe is only an estimate of what the room will be like later.

At the final warm-proof transition, **Proof Check** lets you enter the actual room temperature. Impasto then adjusts the proof guidance so you can react to the conditions in your kitchen rather than blindly following the clock.

## Flour recommendations

Flour guidance changes with the pizza style, hydration, fermentation length, dough method and region.

Impasto recommends an appropriate flour strength and explains why it suits the recipe.

## Flavour and texture guidance

Impasto also explains how choices such as hydration, preferment, sourdough environment, fermentation pace and dough geometry are expected to affect the finished pizza.

These are guidance cues, not guarantees. The clock is the plan; the dough gets the final say.

## Il Risultato

Completed bakes can be logged in **Il Risultato**, Impasto's bake journal.

You can record:

- ⭐ ratings
- 📝 tasting and process notes
- 📷 photos

Saved recipes can build a history of repeated bakes, making it easier to compare results and refine a recipe over time.

## Active, Recent and Saved

Impasto keeps your bakes organised as:

- **Active** — pizzas currently scheduled
- **Recent** — completed bakes
- **Saved** — recipes you want to keep and use again

A Saved recipe can be used again with a new bake time, generating a fresh schedule while keeping its bake history.

## Reminders

Impasto can notify you about meaningful hands-on steps in the timeline, including mixing, folds, fridge transitions, dividing, panning, shaping and baking.

## Sharing

Recipes can be shared as:

- clean recipe text
- a branded visual recipe card

Friends can use the recipe while creating their own exact schedule in Impasto.

## Offline use and backups

Core recipe planning works offline and does not require an account.

Recipes, journal entries and photos are stored on the device, and Impasto supports full backup and restore.
